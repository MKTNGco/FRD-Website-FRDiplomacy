# Who Blogs Here?

     Oct 27  Written By [John Morehead](https://www.frdiplomacy.org/blog?author=58891f6a1b631b179b508ad1)

For the most part the leadership team at FRD will blog here. \John Morehead and Randall Paul will be frequent contributors. FRD includes a group of Senior Advisors (see elsewhere on the FRD website) that might post from time to time. When FRD chapter directors desire to speak to a broader audience than folks in their own tradition, they will post here too. Indeed, we welcome anyone to contact John or Randall (see contact elsewhere on the FRD website) if they want to contribute here, perhaps in an open conversation with us. We won’t guarantee to publish you, but we welcome your initiatives.
